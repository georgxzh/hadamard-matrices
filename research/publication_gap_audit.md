## Defensible contribution and decision

The precise candidate is an auditable **conditional lifting dataset and software
workflow** for a fixed quadratic-character compression: complete solution fibers
above 1,164 intermediate translation classes at p=5, their multiplicities and
incidence with 63 classes under the published equivalence, plus five individually
exhausted p=7 fibers (four empty, one positive). Rational spectral enclosures,
explicit symmetry maps, exact matching, and separate implementation/toolchain
checks make this a useful validation package. These are candidate artifact and
expository contributions; first-publication priority has not been established.

The evidence supports developing a **narrow reproducibility/software paper**,
not a claim of a new length, first classification, first lifting/PSD/matching
algorithm, competitive literature speed, or general complexity advance.
Submission readiness remains unsupported. More optimization or another selected
p=7 branch would not close the principal literature and historical-data gaps.
Broader predetermined p=7 experiments are necessary only for broader statistical
or scaling claims. No p=37 search or proximity claim is made.

Established: PAF/PSD and compression identities, the character prescription,
successive lifting, equivalence reductions, and complementary-vector matching.
Independent reproductions: published LP(45)/LP(63) witnesses and our restricted
counts using separately written local enumerators. Potential original value:
public, explicit conditional fibers including empty intermediates, complete
multiplicity data, branch/class incidence, and an exact-arithmetic reference
implementation. The rectangle specialization's priority remains unresolved.

hashes are saved. The only portability accommodation is `-include string`:

No frozen C++ source is changed. This reuses a separately written auditor from
the existing project; it is **not a newly independent third implementation**.
